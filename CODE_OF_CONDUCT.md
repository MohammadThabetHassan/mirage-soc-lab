# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MIRAGE welcomes constructive collaboration from people of every background, experience level, and identity. We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and security-conscious project environment.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate professionally, give technically grounded feedback, respect the controlled defensive scope of the project, protect sensitive information, and assume good intent while discussing design or security tradeoffs.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, publication of secrets or sensitive telemetry, and contributions intended to enable unauthorized access or harm are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository’s security contact route. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other proportionate actions to protect contributors and the project.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and project-related community spaces.
